# Valio Aimo and agentic-commerce context (web research, Sat 14 Nov 2026)

Gathered by a research agent for the pitch and the UI copy. Every claim has a source; anything marked *unverified* must not appear as fact in the pitch.

## Facts we can use
1. Valio Aimo started in 2021 as a partnership between Valio and Heinon Tukku (not a merger). It is a wholesaler and a manufacturer, serving "from Hanko to Nuorgam". https://www.valioaimo.fi/valio-aimo-in-english.md
2. Delivery wholesale: ~25,000 products, delivered within 24 h on **agreed delivery days**. Warehouses in Espoo and Kuopio, temperature-controlled. Customers: restaurants, hotels, cafés, caterers, large kitchens. https://www.valioaimo.fi/en/become-a-customer/delivery-wholesale.md
3. Ordering channels: the delivery webshop (Toimitustukku), with multi-date carts, edits and recommendations; VALO Tilauspalvelu, multi-producer ordering (Atria, HKFoods, Saarioinen, Snellman Pro, Valio). https://www.valioaimo.fi/en/company/why-choose-valio-aimo-as-a-partner/innovative-tools-for-everyday-work/
4. They already run an AI assistant, **A.I.mo**, in My Aimo (purchase reports, favourites). My Aimo also shows margin calculators and the carbon footprint of purchases. (same source)
5. Sustainability: zero CO₂ in the wholesale business by 2030, half the food waste by 2030. My Aimo shows carbon footprint, domestic-origin share and certified share. https://www.valioaimo.fi/en/responsibility.md (headcounts in the 2025 review: *unverified*)
6. Security: after a breach that came in through an IT service partner's account (~70,000 records, reported around the turn of 2025–26), Valio Aimo warns customers about unexpected requests to change orders, deliveries or invoicing. https://yle.fi/a/74-20139575. Mention gently, if at all.
7. Gartner predicts 90% of B2B buying through AI agents by 2028 (>$15T). https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/11/28/gartner-ai-agents-15-trillion-in-b2b-purchases-by-2028/
8. Foodservice peers: Sysco's SAGE helps product discovery; US Foods' AI tools "do not place orders autonomously". Routine reordering is seen as the right place for agents. https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/02/how-agentic-commerce-is-starting-to-reach-wholesale-distribution/
9. Payment networks are building the trust layer: Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect (Apr 2026, pilot; spend controls, Trusted Agent Protocol); Google AP2 and Mastercard Agent Pay use signed "mandates". https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Opens-the-Door-to-AI-Driven-Shopping-for-Businesses-Worldwide/
10. Burnt (YC, $3.8M seed) turns messy email/WhatsApp/fax orders into ERP entries. https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/25/steph-currys-vc-firm-just-backed-an-ai-startup-that-wants-to-fix-food-supply-chains/

## What this means for us
- **Position the front door as the next step for A.I.mo and the webshop:** the door Valio would offer buyers' own agents. Don't imply a real integration; the API is our proposal (there's no public Valio API or EDI).
- **Autonomy is the industry's sticking point**, so our "agent acts alone only when sure, otherwise asks the chef" fits exactly. Never pitch "fully autonomous".
- Cut-off times and Lapland delivery days in the demo are **demo assumptions**; Valio publishes only "agreed delivery days" and 24 h delivery.
- Demo ideas: (a) an allergy case where "lactose-free" is not "milk-free" for a guest with a milk-protein allergy, caught by the door; (b) an order-tampering attempt (planted text tries to change delivery or invoicing), refused and sent to the chef; (c) carbon and origin per line, matching what My Aimo already reports.
- Pitch: 3 minutes on stage. Judges ask who the user is, who pays, what's real vs mocked, and what's next. Record a backup demo video.
